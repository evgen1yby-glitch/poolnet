Questions

The short version

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free: access has no expiry and no payment.

What if a node drops?

The app checks the connection and automatically switches to the next available gateway.

Do I need an account?

No. The app does not ask for an email, phone or profile.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app is currently available for Android.

Why not from Google Play?

We distribute the APK from this site. Android warns about installing from an unknown source — that is expected.

Is it safe?

Nodes are public and third-party. Do not use them for sensitive data. PoolNet is not the node operator.

How do I update?

Open Profile → Check for updates. The app will show the new version and offer to install it. Your settings stay in place.
