How we verify

Channel sources: iptv-org/iptv, iptv-org/api, Free-TV/IPTV and ngrch/iptv. Listing is not a recommendation or a guarantee that a stream is lawful in your jurisdiction.

01Playlist

Load public M3U sources and merge channels by tvg-id and normalised name.

02Manifest

Check master/media playlists: M3U8, quality variants and no ENDLIST.

03Segment

Fetch the first bytes of the last segment and verify it is video, not HTML.

04Browser

For inline playback also require HTTPS and CORS.
