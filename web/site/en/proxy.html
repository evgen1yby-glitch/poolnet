LEGAL FRAMEWORK

A catalog, not an operator

The connection is established directly between the client and the node. We publish verified addresses from open lists and do not participate in traffic transmission.

BOUNDARY

what the pool should not be used for

The node operator is responsible for actions on the nodes. For sensitive data, use the platform VPN configurations.
